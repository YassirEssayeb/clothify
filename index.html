    Returns & Exchanges Policy

    At Clothify, your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, we're here to help.

    Return Window

    You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return or exchange any unworn, unwashed items with original tags attached.

    Condition Requirements

    • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and free of any stains, odors, or damage
    • All original tags must be attached
    • Footwear must be returned in the original box (use a shipping box to protect the original box)
    • Intimate apparel and swimwear must have hygienic liners intact

    How to Initiate a Return

    Contact our customer service team through the feedback form or email us at returns@clothify.com with your order number and reason for return.

    Refund Process

    Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund within 5-7 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

    • Cash on Delivery: Refund via bank transfer within 7-10 business days
    • Card payments: Refund to your card within 5-7 business days

    Exchange Policy

    Exchanges for different sizes or colors are free of charge. We'll ship the replacement item once the returned item is received and inspected.

    Non-Returnable Items

    • Sale items (final sale)
    • Personalized or custom-made items
    • Gift cards
    • Items worn or damaged after delivery

    Shipping Costs

    Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items. For standard returns, a flat fee of €5 will be deducted from your refund.
